Smart Shopping Guides

Explore all 12 buying guides

How to Spot a Real Deal

Compare the exact model number across retailers. Check historical prices when available, and include shipping and taxes before deciding whether a discount represents genuine savings.

When to Buy Electronics

Compare previous-generation models with newer releases. Older models can offer better value when the newer features do not improve your everyday use. Always check warranty and return policies.

Buying Tools on a Budget

Choose tools based on the projects you actually complete. If you already own cordless batteries, compare compatible bare tools before buying another complete battery and charger kit.

Avoid Impulse Purchases

Set a target price before shopping. A large advertised discount does not necessarily make a product worth buying. Compare alternatives and consider how frequently you will use the item.
