When Buying in Bulk Actually Saves Money

Unit pricing

Compare prices per ounce, pound or individual item rather than relying on package size or advertised savings.

Waste

A lower unit price does not save money if products expire or go unused before you finish them.

Storage

Consider available pantry, refrigerator and freezer space. Extra storage requirements can reduce the benefits of bulk buying.

Memberships

Include warehouse membership fees, transportation and delivery charges when comparing total annual savings.

Buying strategy

Track products your household regularly consumes. Bulk purchases are most useful for predictable needs and products with adequate shelf life.
