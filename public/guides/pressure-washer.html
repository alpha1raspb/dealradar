Pressure Washer Buying Guide

Cleaning needs

Identify whether you will clean vehicles, outdoor furniture, siding or concrete. Different surfaces require different cleaning approaches.

Electric or gas

Electric models can be convenient for smaller household jobs. Gas models may provide greater mobility and cleaning capacity.

Water flow

Compare both pressure and water flow rather than judging performance by pressure alone.

Accessories

Check hose length, nozzle options and compatibility with surface cleaners. Replacement accessories affect long-term ownership costs.

Safety

Follow manufacturer instructions and use suitable pressure for each surface. Never direct a pressure washer at people or animals.
