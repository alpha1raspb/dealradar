RC Cars and Trucks for Beginners

Use this guide to understand important purchasing considerations before comparing available products.

Choose an appropriate scale

Smaller RC vehicles can be easier to transport and operate in limited spaces. Larger vehicles may require more room and larger batteries.

Look for beginner-friendly controls

Proportional steering and adjustable speed can help new drivers develop control. Consider ready-to-run models for simpler setup.

Check replacement parts

Availability of tires, suspension components and batteries matters because crashes and normal wear are common in RC driving.
