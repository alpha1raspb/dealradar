How to Choose a Budget Laptop

Performance

For everyday browsing, documents and video calls, prioritize a modern processor and at least 8GB of RAM. Consider 16GB if you regularly multitask or keep many browser tabs open.

Storage

Choose solid-state storage over a traditional hard drive. A 256GB SSD may be sufficient for basic use, while 512GB provides more room for applications and personal files.

Display

A 1080p display is a practical starting point. Check brightness, viewing angles and keyboard comfort before purchasing.

Hidden costs

Compare warranty coverage, operating system requirements and included accessories. Refurbished business laptops can offer good value when backed by a reliable warranty.

Buying strategy

Compare exact model numbers rather than relying on advertised discounts. Avoid paying extra for gaming graphics unless you actually need them.
