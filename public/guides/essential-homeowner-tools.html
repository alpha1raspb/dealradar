Essential Tools for Homeowners

Use this guide to understand important purchasing considerations before comparing available products.

Start with frequently used tools

A cordless drill, screwdriver set, tape measure and adjustable wrench cover many common household maintenance tasks.

Consider battery compatibility

Buying tools within one cordless battery platform can reduce future costs. Check whether batteries and chargers are included.

Buy for your actual projects

An occasional homeowner may not need professional equipment. Compare intended use, warranty and included accessories before purchasing.
