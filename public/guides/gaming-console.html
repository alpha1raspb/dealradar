Xbox vs PlayStation: Getting the Best Value

Games

Start by identifying the games you actually want to play. Exclusive titles and the platforms your friends use can influence the best choice.

Subscriptions

Compare subscription costs, included games and online multiplayer requirements. Recurring expenses can exceed the initial hardware savings.

Storage

Large modern games require considerable storage. Compare expansion options and their prices before buying.

Disc or digital

Disc-compatible consoles may allow you to purchase used games and resell physical copies. Digital consoles rely on digital storefront pricing.

Buying strategy

Compare the total cost of the console, games, subscriptions and accessories over several years instead of focusing only on the console price.
