Gaming Chairs Under $350

Use this guide to understand important purchasing considerations before comparing available products.

Prioritize fit over appearance

Compare seat width, seat depth, height adjustment and manufacturer weight limits. A chair should fit both your body and your desk.

Compare upholstery materials

Mesh, fabric and synthetic leather offer different levels of ventilation, texture and maintenance. Choose based on your room temperature and preferences.

Review adjustment options

Adjustable armrests, reclining mechanisms and lumbar support may improve comfort when properly fitted. Check return policies before purchasing.
