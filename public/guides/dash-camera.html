Dash Camera Buying Guide

Video quality

Compare sample footage in daylight and at night. Resolution alone does not guarantee readable details.

Coverage

A front camera provides basic coverage. Front and rear systems may provide additional context during an incident.

Storage

Choose a compatible memory card designed for continuous recording. Check the manufacturer's capacity requirements.

Parking mode

Parking monitoring may require additional wiring or power management equipment. Include installation costs in your budget.

Privacy

Check applicable recording and windshield mounting laws. Review audio recording settings and protect stored footage.
