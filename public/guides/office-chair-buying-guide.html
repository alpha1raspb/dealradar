How to Choose an Office Chair

Use this guide to understand important purchasing considerations before comparing available products.

Start with seat adjustment

Look for a seat height that allows your feet to rest comfortably on the floor or a footrest. Seat depth should suit your leg length.

Consider back support

Adjustable lumbar support and backrest positioning can help accommodate different sitting preferences. Mesh may offer greater ventilation.

Check desk compatibility

Measure your available space and desk clearance. Consider armrest height, chair width and the type of flooring beneath your chair.
