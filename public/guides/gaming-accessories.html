How to Save Money on Gaming Accessories

Controllers

Check platform compatibility and warranty coverage. An inexpensive controller may be suitable for occasional multiplayer sessions.

Headsets

Prioritize comfort, microphone clarity and compatibility over decorative lighting or unnecessary features.

Charging

Compare rechargeable battery options and charging stations against the ongoing cost of disposable batteries.

Storage

Verify that external drives meet your console requirements. Some drives can store games but cannot run every game directly.

Buying strategy

Purchase accessories as needed instead of buying large bundles containing products you may never use.
