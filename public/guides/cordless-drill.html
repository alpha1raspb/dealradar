Cordless Drill Buying Guide

Battery platform

If you already own cordless tools, check whether your existing batteries are compatible with the drill you are considering.

Drill or impact driver

A drill is useful for drilling holes and controlled fastening. An impact driver is often better suited to repetitive screw driving.

Power

Compact drills can handle many household projects. Larger tools may be appropriate for demanding applications but add weight.

Kit pricing

Compare bare tools with kits containing batteries and chargers. A discounted kit may offer better value for a first-time buyer.

Safety

Use appropriate bits, eye protection and manufacturer instructions. Check for concealed wiring and plumbing before drilling into walls.
