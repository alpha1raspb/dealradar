Smart TV Buying Guide

Screen size

Measure your available space and viewing distance before selecting a television. Larger screens are not always better for smaller rooms.

Picture quality

Consider contrast, brightness and viewing angles. A television used in a bright living room has different requirements from one used in a dark bedroom.

Gaming

Console gamers should check refresh rate, input lag and HDMI capabilities. Confirm that advertised gaming features work at the resolution you intend to use.

Software

Check whether your preferred streaming services are supported. An external streaming device can extend the usefulness of an older television.

Buying strategy

Compare previous-generation models and include delivery, mounting hardware and warranty coverage in your total budget.
