Choosing a 10x10 Outdoor Canopy

Use this guide to understand important purchasing considerations before comparing available products.

Measure your available space

A nominal 10-by-10-foot canopy covers approximately 100 square feet. Allow additional space for anchoring equipment and access around the shelter.

Compare frame and fabric construction

Check frame material, fabric specifications, water resistance and replacement part availability. Review the manufacturer's intended use.

Plan for safe setup

Follow manufacturer anchoring instructions and weather restrictions. Temporary canopies should not be relied upon as severe-weather shelters.
