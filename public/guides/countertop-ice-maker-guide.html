Countertop Ice Maker Buying Guide

Use this guide to understand important purchasing considerations before comparing available products.

Choose your preferred ice type

Bullet ice and nugget ice have different shapes and textures. Consider whether you primarily want ice for beverages, entertaining or everyday use.

Understand production capacity

Daily production capacity is different from storage capacity. Check how much ice the basket holds and whether the machine provides refrigeration.

Consider cleaning and placement

Check countertop dimensions, ventilation clearance and cleaning instructions. Regular cleaning is important even when a machine has a self-cleaning mode.
