Choosing the Right Leaf Blower

Property size

Small patios and driveways may only require a compact electric blower. Larger properties may benefit from longer runtime.

Air performance

Compare air volume and air speed together. Moving large piles of leaves requires different performance from clearing dust.

Battery compatibility

An existing cordless battery platform can reduce purchase costs and simplify charging.

Comfort

Check weight, balance, handle design and noise levels. These factors become more important during extended use.

Buying strategy

Consider seasonal discounts and compare complete kits against bare tools. Check local noise restrictions before choosing a model.
