Electric Bikes Under $1,500

Use this guide to understand important purchasing considerations before comparing available products.

Choose the right riding style

Consider whether you primarily ride on pavement, gravel trails or mixed terrain. Folding bikes may be convenient for storage, while step-through frames can make mounting and dismounting easier.

Compare battery range

Advertised range depends on rider weight, terrain, temperature and pedal assistance. Check battery capacity, replacement cost and charging time.

Check ownership costs

Budget for a helmet, lock, maintenance and replacement parts. Verify local e-bike rules and manufacturer warranty coverage.
