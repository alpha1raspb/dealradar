How to Choose a Lithium Battery

Use this guide to understand important purchasing considerations before comparing available products.

Match voltage to your equipment

Battery voltage must match the requirements of your equipment. A 12-volt battery and a 48-volt battery system are not interchangeable.

Understand energy capacity

Approximate nominal energy in watt-hours is voltage multiplied by amp-hours. Actual usable energy depends on system limits and operating conditions.

Verify charging and installation requirements

Check battery chemistry, charger compatibility, current limits, temperature restrictions and manufacturer installation instructions. Consult a qualified installer when modifying electrical systems.
